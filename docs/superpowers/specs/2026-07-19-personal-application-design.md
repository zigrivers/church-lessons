# Personal Application Expansion Design

## Goal

Deepen *Trust Under Pressure* with thought-provoking questions that help 17-year-old boys apply the lesson to present-day challenges while preserving the existing 50-minute pacing, visual system, and privacy protections.

## Content design

Add three in-flow application labs. The teacher chooses one question from each lab rather than discussing every prompt.

1. **Faith under pressure** belongs in “The board.” It connects Assyria’s public pressure to classrooms, teams, group chats, online feeds, skeptical friends, and anxious news. Questions distinguish faithful courage from silence, defensiveness, or unkindness.
2. **Hezekiah’s pattern** also belongs in “The board.” It asks students to examine what Hezekiah did before knowing the outcome: refuse to answer intimidation on its terms, go to the Lord, seek prophetic counsel, spread out the real message, pray, and take the next faithful step. It explicitly separates trusting God from passivity.
3. **Scripture and trust** spans “The missing book” and “Read or reform?” It asks whether scripture is allowed to correct current choices, then separates “my faithful part” from outcomes that belong in Heavenly Father’s hands. It anchors hope explicitly in Heavenly Father and Jesus Christ, including when the preferred outcome does not happen.

The prompts remain scenario-based before becoming private. No student is required to disclose a personal doubt, sin, family problem, or private choice.

## Visual design

Reuse the existing Siege-to-Sanctuary system. Each lab is a bordered editorial panel with a small uppercase label, a concise setup, and large question cards. Dark panels belong to pressure; light panels belong to scripture; the final trust panel uses covenant gold and prayer blue. No new animation or interaction is necessary.

## Timing

Each lab says “Choose one” and the teacher notes reinforce a two-minute discussion cap. The new material replaces generic follow-up time rather than increasing the 50-minute total.

The design contains no placeholders or new subsystem. It preserves the approved lesson architecture, explicitly covers every requested principle, and defines how timing and privacy remain intact.
